The day the world got brighter

Chapter Two · Us

Then love said hello

It was never one big, loud moment. It was a hundred small ones: a text at the right time, a laugh that lasted too long, a hand that stayed a second more.

Prince stands a little taller beside her. Gloria glows a little brighter beside him. Together they look like what peace feels like.

Love is not finding someone perfect.
It is choosing the same person, every single day.

Chapter Eight · The Letter

A birthday letter, sealed with love

Tap the envelope.

My dearest Gloria,

Happy birthday, my love. Today I want you to stop being the strong one, the planner, the one who holds everybody together, and just let the world hold you.

You are the kind of woman people feel better around. You walk in, and the room exhales. You laugh, and every worry I carried suddenly feels small.

I cannot promise you a life without hard days. But I promise to meet every one of them beside you, with honesty, patience, respect and a lot of laughter.

Thank you for loving me on my best days and my worst ones. Thank you for being home.

May this year bring you peace that stays, joy that overflows, and love that never has to ask twice.

Forever yours,
Prince ♥

Today, tomorrow & always

Happy Birthday,
Gloria
